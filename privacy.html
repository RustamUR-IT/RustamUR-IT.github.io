PRIVACY POLICY

Veilrise (Android app, Wear OS app and this website) · Effective 28 October 2026

The short version

  • Everything you log stays on your phone. There is no account, no server of ours, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • We, the developer, never receive your data.
  • Data only leaves your phone in the cases listed below, and almost all of them happen because you start them, for example when you share a card or ask Gemini about a meal.

Who is responsible

Rustam (RustamUR-IT), Vienna, Austria · contact: github.com/RustamUR-IT/RustamUR-IT.github.io/issues

What the app stores on your phone

To work, the app saves the following in its private storage on your device. Other apps cannot read it.

  • Profile: name or nickname, sex (and which sex to use for calculations), age, height, body weight, goal, training paths, look of your avatar.
  • Training: sets, workouts, timer sessions, runs (distance, time and pace; no route is kept), Zone 2 sessions with heart rate, records, skills, plans.
  • Body and food: weight entries, body scan values, meals and food you log, water, optional physique photos and meal photos.
  • Health and recovery: values read from Health Connect (see below), sleep, readiness, notes.
  • Game progress: XP, levels, quests, streaks, bosses, seasons, your guild and your friends' hunter cards.
  • Settings: including an optional Gemini API key and an optional backup password. Both are stored encrypted with a key held in your phone's secure hardware (Android Keystore), kept apart from everything else, and never included in backups or exports.
  • Crash reports: if the app crashes, a technical report is saved on your phone. It stays there unless you send it yourself.

Permissions and why the app asks

  • Health Connect (steps, distance, calories, sleep, heart rate, resting heart rate, HRV, exercise, weight, body fat, lean mass): readiness, recovery, calorie target and stats. Optionally writes your finished workouts to Health Connect if you switch that on.
  • Location (precise, only while a run is recording): distance and pace of GPS runs. The route itself is not stored. If you use the Fog of War map, the phone keeps only which ≈150 m cells you ran through, on the phone only, never in backups or exports. The app never reads your location in the background when no run is recording.
  • Bluetooth: connecting a heart-rate chest strap or band. Scanning is not used to find your location.
  • Calendar (read and write, optional): the week planner reads only when you're busy (never titles or details) and can add your planned workouts to a calendar you choose.
  • Notifications, foreground services, wake lock, vibration: so the interval timer, rest timer, run and heart-rate session keep working with the screen off.
  • Display over other apps (optional): a small heart-rate bubble during Zone 2 sessions, and the short pause screen of App friction.
  • Usage access (optional, App friction only): which app is in front, so the pause screen can appear before an app you chose to limit. Checked on the phone only. The app keeps no list of the apps you use; it only saves, in your habit history on the phone, whether you went back or continued at a pause.
  • Camera: the app has no camera permission. The barcode scanner and photo picker are Android and Google Play services screens that only return the result. Google may collect anonymous statistics about the use of its scanner (see Google's ML Kit terms).
  • Internet: only for the cases in the table above.

Health Connect

The use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Health Connect data is processed only on your phone to show your own training and recovery information. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, never used for advertising, and never sold.

No selling, no advertising, no profiling

We do not sell or rent data, show ads, build advertising profiles, or use data for anything except the features you use in the app.

Children

The app is meant for people aged 16 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. Because data stays on the device, we hold no data we could delete. If you are a parent with a question, contact us.

How long data is kept, and how to delete it

  • Data stays on your phone until you delete it. In the app, Settings → Data → Delete all data removes everything at once (you type a word to confirm). Uninstalling the app, or Android Settings → Apps → Veilrise → Storage → Clear data, does the same.
  • Single entries can be deleted in the app. Backup files you created stay in the folder you chose until you delete them.
  • Friends' hunter cards are deleted with “remove from guild”.
  • Copies held by services you used (Google Gemini, Open Food Facts, your cloud storage, the apps you shared to) are covered by those services' policies.

Security

Data is stored in the app's private storage, which Android protects. Connections to Open Food Facts and Gemini use HTTPS. Backups can be encrypted with a password. By default the status notification carries no health values (only level, quests and streak), so none can appear on the lock screen; you can turn them on in Settings → Privacy. Typing your weight into the evening check-in needs the phone unlocked.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict and port your data, and to object. Since we hold no personal data about you, most requests can be met directly in the app: export, edit or delete. For any question, write to the address above. You can also complain to the Austrian Data Protection Authority (Datenschutzbehörde, Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna) or to the authority where you live.

Changes

If the app starts sending data anywhere new, this policy is updated before that version is released, and the app tells you. The date at the top shows the current version.

Speicherdauer und Löschen

  • Daten bleiben auf deinem Handy, bis du sie löschst. In der App entfernt Einstellungen → Daten → Alle Daten löschen alles auf einmal (zur Bestätigung tippst du ein Wort). Deinstallieren oder Android-Einstellungen → Apps → Veilrise → Speicher → Daten löschen bewirkt dasselbe.
  • Einzelne Einträge kannst du in der App löschen. Backup-Dateien bleiben im gewählten Ordner, bis du sie löschst.
  • Hunter-Karten von Freunden löschst du mit „aus der Gilde entfernen“.
  • Kopien bei Diensten, die du genutzt hast (Google Gemini, Open Food Facts, dein Cloud-Speicher, die Apps, an die du geteilt hast), unterliegen deren Richtlinien.

Sicherheit

Die Daten liegen im privaten App-Speicher, den Android schützt. Verbindungen zu Open Food Facts und Gemini laufen über HTTPS. Backups lassen sich mit Passwort verschlüsseln.
