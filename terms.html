2. Health and safety: please read

  • The app is not a medical device and gives no medical advice. Readiness, warnings, calorie targets, heart-rate zones, stats, ranks and training suggestions are general fitness estimates. They can be wrong.
  • Talk to a doctor before you start or change hard training or a diet, and especially if you have pain, an injury, an illness, a heart or blood-pressure condition, an eating disorder, if you are pregnant, or if you take medication.
  • Stop right away if you feel pain, dizziness, chest pain or shortness of breath, and get medical help if needed.
  • Train within your limits, warm up, use safe technique and equipment, and use a spotter for heavy lifts. Game goals (bosses, streaks, rankings) are motivation, never a reason to train hurt or sick.
  • During runs, watch the road and traffic, not your phone.
